## Tips on naming

- **Keep it short** — 1–2 words is easier to read on cards than 3+
- **Avoid clichés** — "Professional", "Best", "Premium", "Clean", "Modern" feel generic if used as-is in the name; pair them with something specific
- **Single-word names feel strongest** — Apex, Helm, Quartz, Saffron, Stratus
- **Year/edition tags work well for the "Recent 2026" section** but feel dated everywhere else
- **Match the name to the design vibe** — Pinstripe and Boardroom for formal, Saffron and Carnival for creative, Linen and Mist for minimalist
